Ecosystem

The Triadic Cosmos GitHub Project

The Triadic Cosmos is a unified intellectual ecosystem that develops a structural, architectural, and modular understanding of intelligence, narration, and physical reality. Across books, papers, and runnable toy universes, the ecosystem builds a coherent framework grounded in one central idea: complex systems— from language to minds to stories to the universe itself—emerge from the recursive interaction of three architectural primitives: energy, information, and geometry.

The ecosystem now spans three major domains—Intelligence, Narration, and Physics—each developed through modular components that interlock into a single conceptual structure. Together, they form a comprehensive theory of how systems grow, stabilise, reason, tell stories, and evolve under explicit architectural constraints.

Intelligence

The intelligence branch develops a fully modular, interpretable, and safe architecture for artificial reasoning systems. It introduces Dynamic Modular Language Graphs (DMLGs) and a complete AGI architecture based on paging, evaluators, and recursive PRE loops. These components complement monolithic transformers with a structured, deterministic, and governable alternative.

Research in this branch covers:

  • Language as Graph Traversal — grammar, semantics, and world models emerge from traversing modular language graphs rather than sampling from dense vector spaces.
  • Dynamic Modular Language Models — a new class of language models composed of many small, domain‑bound micro‑models governed by explicit evaluators.
  • Safe and Modular AGI — a full architecture (Paged‑PRE‑CAS) where reasoning is transparent, traceable, and structurally constrained.
  • Structural Limits of AI Scaling — an analysis of why monolithic LLMs face inherent scaling barriers and why modularity is required for long‑range coherence.
  • Emergence of Intelligence — a structural taxonomy showing how biological, artificial, and hybrid intelligences arise from the same triadic architecture.
  • Toy Universes — runnable micro‑worlds that demonstrate emergence, modular reasoning, and structural constraints in interpretable settings.

Narration

The narration branch develops a modular, end‑to‑end architecture for autonomous book generation and synthetic worlds. It treats stories as dynamical systems grown through recursive pipelines rather than single‑pass generation, combining DMLG governance, hybrid models, and staged refinement into a transparent narrator architecture.

Research in this branch covers:

  • Tales of a Sophisticated Parrot — hybrid IEGR systems that combine small LLMs with DMLG world models to produce long‑form literature at microscopic scale.
  • Diffusion‑Driven Narration — staged denoising across heterogeneous modules, where narrative structure diffuses through multiple cognitive layers.
  • The Dark Forest & The Shrouded City — fully autonomous narrative organisms grown through recursive remastering, director’s‑cut refinement, and attractor stabilization.
  • Narrator Pipelines — structural hybrid‑intelligence architectures where governance, recursion, and modularity dominate generation.

Physics

The physics branch develops a structural ontology of reality grounded in the Triadic Cycle: a closed interdependence between energy, geometry, and information. This cycle appears across quantum mechanics, general relativity, thermodynamics, holography, and cosmology, revealing a unified architectural pattern underlying physical law. Recent work establishes a full architectural foundation for unification, linking GR, QFT, the Standard Model, AdS/CFT, EP=EPR, RG flow, and observational constraints into a single triadic framework.

Research in this branch covers:

  • The Triadic Cycle — the foundational ontological structure showing how physical phenomena arise from the recursive interaction of the three primitives.
  • Structural Principles for Unification — architectural constraints that any viable unifying physical theory must satisfy.
  • AdS/CFT and Holography — how holographic dualities instantiate the triadic structure across scales.
  • The Universe as Recursive Triadic Fractal — a cosmological model where physical, chemical, biological, and cognitive structures emerge as fractal repetitions of the same triadic architecture.
  • The Architecture of Reality & Foundation for Unification — syntheses showing how energy, geometry, and information generate the observable universe through recursive constraint propagation and triadic closure.

Unified Vision

Together, the intelligence, narration, and physics branches form a single architectural worldview: minds, stories, and universes are not separate categories but different scales of the same triadic, recursive, modular structure. The Triadic Cosmos ecosystem provides the conceptual foundations, mathematical principles, computational architectures, and experimental demonstrations needed to explore this unified perspective.
