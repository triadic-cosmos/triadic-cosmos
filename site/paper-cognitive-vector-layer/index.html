Introduction

Generative language systems are traditionally driven by large-scale statistical models whose behavior is shaped by textual prompting, sampling noise, and implicit correlations learned from vast datasets. While these systems can produce fluent prose, their internal dynamics remain opaque, their stylistic control coarse, and their generative behavior constrained by the statistical closure of their training data. Architectural Intelligence Theory proposes an alternative paradigm: cognitive behavior can emerge not from scale or prompting, but from the interaction of modular components whose internal structure defines new representational manifolds. In this view, intelligence is an architectural property rather than a statistical artifact.

This paper presents a compact, operational instance of such an architecture by extending the GLP–Graph (GLPG) narrator engine with the Cognitive Vector Layer (CAS). GLPG is a deterministic, graph-based narrator model that generates machine-native lemma sequences, pacing arcs, and canonical cadences learned from a small dataset of four stylistically distinct worlds. CAS provides a lightweight modulation signal—four scalar values concatenated to the GLPG context vector—that acts as a geometric operator over the GLPG manifold. Despite its minimal informational density, CAS induces strong stylistic curvature, activates manifold regions, blends attractors, and produces emergent narrative behaviors far beyond the closure of the training corpus.

The resulting CAS × GLPG system forms a promptless, vector-driven generative architecture. GLPG supplies deterministic canonical structure; CAS supplies geometric modulation; the narrator pipeline provides multi-level PRE (propose–retry) recursion; and a lightweight moderator LLM translates GLPG’s internal narrator language into coherent chapters using short, stable prompts. This separation of roles yields a modular, inspectable, and energetically efficient system whose behavior is governed by architectural composition rather than stochastic prompting.

Empirically, the system exhibits closure-breaking generation: CAS-driven manifold warping produces stylistic attractors, hybrid worlds, and emergent authors not present in the dataset but structurally consistent with GLPG’s canonical geometry. Extreme CAS vectors, including [106,106,106,106] and [−106,−106,−106,−106], reveal saturation and inversion regimes in which CAS dominates the generative physics of the system. These behaviors demonstrate that architectural modulation can unlock new representational manifolds without increasing model scale or relying on complex prompts.

The remainder of this paper develops the architectural, empirical, and theoretical foundations of CAS × GLPG. We introduce CAS as a personality and content modulation layer; describe its integration with GLPG; analyze its behavior through ablation studies; map the resulting stylistic manifold in The Parrot Family: A CAS Personality Atlas; and evaluate the system against the ten criteria of Architectural Intelligence Theory. Together, these results show that extending GLPG with CAS yields a domain-bound Architectural General Intelligence: a compact generative system capable of controlled personality modulation, emergent stylistic variation, and closure-breaking narrative generation.

CAS: Cognitive Vectors for Content and Personality Modulation

The Cognitive Vector Layer (CAS) was originally introduced in the AGI architecture as a general epistemic control mechanism for multi-dimensional evaluation. In its full formulation, CAS attaches a structured vector to every intermediate and final candidate, encoding orthogonal cognitive dimensions such as safety, accuracy, relevance, style, and domain coherence. These vectors guide the PRE refinement loop by providing explicit targets for evaluators and refiners, transforming generation from an unguided recursive process into a controlled cognitive trajectory.

In the present work, CAS is used in a more focused role: modulating content and personality. Rather than employing the full epistemic geometry, we restrict CAS to the dimensions that directly influence narrative intent, stylistic expression, and character-driven modulation. This specialization allows CAS to act as a high-level personality controller layered on top of GLPG and the narrator pipeline, enabling fine-grained stylistic steering without altering the underlying structural or semantic mechanisms.

Content and Personality Vectors

A CAS vector in this context encodes two primary dimensions:

  • content: thematic alignment, narrative focus, and semantic intent,

  • personality: tone, affect, cadence, and stylistic coloration.

These dimensions operate independently and can be combined to produce complex modulation patterns. For example, a high-intensity personality vector can enforce a distinctive narrative voice, while a neutral content vector maintains canonical story structure. Conversely, a strong content vector can enforce thematic constraints while personality remains subtle or absent.

Orthogonality and Controlled Modulation

CAS maintains strict orthogonality between content and personality. This separation ensures that stylistic modulation does not interfere with semantic coherence, and that thematic constraints do not distort the intended narrative voice. Orthogonality provides:

  • predictability: personality changes do not alter plot structure,

  • stability: content constraints do not collapse stylistic variation,

  • composability: vectors can be combined or swapped without cross-dimensional interference.

This design mirrors the original CAS principle that evaluation dimensions should remain disentangled to preserve interpretability and modularity.

CAS-Guided Personality Shaping

During generation, the proposer conditions candidates on a target CAS vector, embedding content and personality goals directly into the sampling process. The evaluators compute alignment scores for both dimensions, and the refiner adjusts the candidate to better match the intended cognitive profile. This creates a closed-loop control system in which personality becomes a first-class modulation signal rather than an emergent side effect of prompt engineering.

Relation to the Original CAS Architecture

While the original CAS formulation includes safety, accuracy, relevance, and domain coherence, these dimensions are not central to the present application. The narrative pipeline already enforces structural correctness and canonical closure through GLPG, and safety/accuracy evaluators are not required for stylistic modulation. CAS therefore functions here as a reduced cognitive layer, retaining its orthogonality and control properties while specializing in content and personality shaping.

This specialization demonstrates that CAS is not limited to epistemic evaluation but can serve as a general-purpose cognitive modulation mechanism. In this paper, CAS provides the vector geometry through which personality becomes a controllable, continuous parameter rather than a discrete stylistic mode.

CAS Vectors as Personality Modulators in GLPG Training

In this work, CAS is used in a concrete and operational form: each book in the training corpus is assigned a dedicated scalar dimension in a CAS vector. For a dataset of N books, the CAS vector has dimension N, and each book corresponds to exactly one float within this vector. During training, every sentence extracted from a given book is paired with a one-hot CAS vector in which the dimension associated with that book is set to 1 and all others to 0. This creates a consistent and high-intensity modulation signal that the model can reliably learn to associate with stylistic and narrative properties.

Concatenation with the GLPG Context Vector

The GLPG model used in this paper employs a context input vector of size 2000. The CAS vector is concatenated directly to this context representation, producing a combined input vector of size 2000 + N. For the present dataset of four books, the resulting input dimensionality is 2004. Although the CAS component is small relative to the full input vector, its one-hot structure and perfect consistency across all sentences of a given book make it an extremely salient training signal. The model therefore learns to treat CAS as a stable personality indicator layered on top of the standard GLPG context features.

Learning Book-Specific Personality Profiles

Because every sentence of a book is paired with the same CAS vector, the model implicitly learns a personality profile for each book. These profiles emerge from the stylistic, structural, and thematic regularities present in the book’s text. The CAS vector acts as a selector that activates the corresponding personality during generation. At inference time, supplying a CAS vector allows the model to produce controlled stylistic variations while maintaining the underlying GLPG structural coherence.

Extending CAS Beyond One-Hot Book Labels

The one-hot formulation used here represents the simplest instantiation of CAS. More expressive CAS vectors can be constructed by assigning high-dimensional content and personality descriptors to each book. These descriptors may encode attributes such as tone, cadence, affect, narrative density, or thematic focus. Training with such enriched CAS vectors would allow the model to learn a high-dimensional personality manifold, enabling fine-grained modulation during generation. Increasing the number of books further expands the diversity of examples the model observes, strengthening its ability to generalize personality control across the CAS space.

Controlled Variation Within a Single GLPG Model

This approach demonstrates that CAS provides a lightweight yet powerful mechanism for controlled variation within a single GLPG model. By attaching a small, interpretable vector to the input, the model can be trained to produce multiple distinct narrative styles without requiring separate models or complex prompt engineering. CAS therefore functions as a modular personality interface, enabling continuous and composable stylistic control during generation.

CAS × GLPG as a Vector-Driven Promptless Search Mechanism

The Cognitive Vector Layer (CAS) can be interpreted as a lightweight vector database that interfaces directly with the GLPG generative model. In this view, CAS provides a continuous, queryable representation of stylistic and cognitive intent, while GLPG acts as the decoder that transforms these vectors into coherent narrative output. This establishes a promptless search-and-generation mechanism in which vector composition replaces textual prompting as the primary control signal.

CAS as a Vector Database

A CAS vector represents a point in a high-dimensional cognitive space. Each dimension corresponds to a learned stylistic or personality factor, and the vector as a whole functions as a query into the model’s internal manifold of narrative behaviors. Unlike traditional vector databases that retrieve stored embeddings, CAS operates as a generative vector database: the query does not retrieve an existing item but instead produces a new output whose properties match the target vector.

This mechanism allows CAS to serve as an internal prompt generator. Rather than constructing a textual prompt that describes the desired style or personality, the user supplies a CAS vector, and the system interprets this vector as a precise, machine-native description of the intended cognitive state.

Promptless Cognitive Search

CAS × GLPG therefore implements a form of promptless cognitive search. The CAS vector acts as a search key that selects a region of the model’s latent narrative manifold. GLPG then decodes this region into text, producing output that reflects the stylistic, affective, and structural properties encoded in the vector. This process bypasses the need for explicit textual prompting and eliminates the ambiguity inherent in natural-language instructions.

Formally, generation becomes:

output = GLPG(context ‖ CAS),

where the CAS vector determines the cognitive modulation applied during decoding. The model effectively performs a vector-driven search over its internal stylistic space, guided by the CAS signal.

Capabilities Beyond Conventional LLM Prompting

This architecture enables capabilities that are difficult or impossible for conventional LLMs:

  • precise modulation: CAS provides a deterministic and interpretable control signal, unlike textual prompts which are ambiguous and context-dependent,

  • continuous variation: personality and style can be interpolated or blended by manipulating CAS vectors,

  • compositional control: multiple stylistic factors can be combined linearly, enabling emergent hybrid styles,

  • internal prompting: the model receives a machine-native prompt that requires no linguistic interpretation.

This leads to the central observation of this paper:

CAS × GLPG is a vector-database that automatically generates an internal, machine-native prompt for a text generator—achieving a form of controlled generation that no current LLM system can simulate, and which would require extensive cognitive effort for a human to construct manually.

Emergent Personality Control Through Vector Composition

Because CAS vectors can be composed, interpolated, or scaled, the system supports emergent personality control. By adjusting the CAS vector, the user can steer the model toward different stylistic attractors without modifying the underlying GLPG architecture or relying on prompt engineering. This makes CAS × GLPG a modular and extensible mechanism for controlled narrative variation within a single generative model.

In summary, CAS provides the vector geometry, GLPG provides the decoder, and their combination yields a promptless, vector-driven generative interface that functions as a cognitive search mechanism over the model’s internal stylistic manifold.

CAS × GLPG as an Empirical Instance of Architectural Intelligence

The Architectural Intelligence Theory proposes that cognitive systems can break the closure of their training data through architectural composition rather than scale, prompting, or statistical generalization. A system achieves closure breaking when its internal components interact to produce new representational manifolds that do not exist in the dataset but remain structurally coherent within the system’s own constraints. The CAS × GLPG architecture presented in this paper constitutes a concrete, lightweight empirical example of such closure-breaking behavior within a domain-bound generative intelligence: fictional narration.

Architectural Composition of CAS and GLPG

CAS × GLPG combines two architecturally distinct modules:

  • CAS: a vectorial personality and style space functioning as a semantic vector-database of narrative attractors, syntactic fields, stylistic manifolds, and domain cues,

  • GLPG: a deterministic generative engine that decodes these vectors into coherent prose using lemma clusters, pacing chains, cadential profiles, and canon structures learned during training.

During training, each world (book) forms its own manifold region inside GLPG: clusters of lemmas, stable cadences, tone fields, and structural canon patterns. CAS provides a numerical vector that activates these regions in different weights, allowing GLPG to form blended latent states that do not necessarily correspond to any single style present in the dataset. The resulting narrative output emerges from linear algebra over multiple world vectors, including derivative worlds such as The Hybrid Parrot, which arise emergently yet function as basis vectors for further composition.

Vectorial Canon Composition

This architecture enables a form of vectorial canon composition: new narrative forms are generated by combining manifold regions through CAS vectors rather than through textual prompting. The system therefore acts as a modular, extensible style engine capable of producing novel, canon-consistent narrative behaviors. CAS × GLPG operates as a vector-driven promptless search mechanism in which CAS serves as an internal query into the stylistic manifold, and GLPG provides the deterministic decoding.

This mechanism is architecturally unique. It is not transformer-like, not token-predictive, not RLHF-guided, and not dependent on large-scale datasets for stylistic variation. Yet it maintains canon consistency, stylistic stability, world coherence, and emergent variation across attractors. The variability does not arise from sampling noise but from architectural interaction between CAS and GLPG.

Closure Breaking Through Architectural Interaction

According to Architectural Intelligence Theory, closure breaking occurs when a system generates new manifolds outside the statistical closure of its dataset while remaining structurally constrained by its architecture. CAS × GLPG exhibits this property:

  • GLPG provides a fixed representational closure: a deterministic narrator pipeline, fixed prompts, no meta-reasoning, and no external knowledge.

  • CAS acts as an architectural intervention that warps and selects manifold regions, enabling new attractors and emergent narrative dynamics.

  • The interaction between CAS and GLPG produces authors, styles, and narrative worlds not present in the dataset but consistent within the system’s structural canon.

The LLM used in the narrator pipeline does not generate the manifold; it merely expresses the GLPG states linguistically. It cannot predict or correct the structural output of GLPG, and therefore the emergent behavior originates entirely from the architectural composition. This aligns precisely with the theoretical claim that architecture—not scale—can open new representational manifolds.

Architectural General Intelligence in a Domain-Bound Setting

Architectural General Intelligence (AGI) is defined in the theory as a software stack whose internal components collectively produce cognitive behavior that is challenging for general intelligence and unattainable for hyperscaled frontier models. CAS × GLPG demonstrates this in a domain-bound setting: vectorial style composition, emergent agency, canon-consistent narration, and architectural cognition.

These capabilities exceed what current LLMs can simulate. Human authors can master fragments of this skill—Joyce for cadence, Borges for conceptual manifolds, Calvino for multi-style composition—but no human can maintain four distinct styles, lemma fields, cadential profiles, and canon structures in modular, vectorial, and consistent combination. CAS × GLPG therefore extends the human narrative manifold through architectural means.

Empirical Confirmation of the Theory

The system presented here provides an empirical demonstration of the core principle of Architectural Intelligence Theory:

Architecture can break closure by generating new representational manifolds that are not derived from the dataset but are constrained by the system’s internal structure.

CAS × GLPG is thus not merely a stylistic engine but a compact, architecturally-driven cognitive system whose outputs constitute manifold traces of a closure-breaking generative intelligence.

Dataset and Canon Profiles

The experiments in this paper use a compact but stylistically diverse dataset consisting of four narrative worlds. Each world corresponds to one dimension in the CAS vector, and every sentence extracted from a given world is paired with a one-hot CAS vector. This allows the model to learn distinct manifold regions for each world and to modulate them through CAS-driven vector composition.

Source Worlds

The dataset contains four books representing four stylistic and canonical domains:

  • The Hybrid Parrot (Iterative Narrator Pipeline) — a synthetic hybrid world composed of Alice in Wonderland, The Strange Case of Dr. Jekyll and Mr. Hyde, and Metamorphosis. This world is an existing ecosystem artifact rather than a purely human-designed domain.

  • Astronomy for Amateurs (Camille Flammarion) — a descriptive, observational, cosmically oriented non-fiction domain.

  • The Picture of Dorian Gray (Oscar Wilde) — a decadent, introspective, emotionally charged fictional domain.

  • A Honeymoon in Space (George Chetwynd Griffith) — a pulp science-fiction domain with poetic cadence and relational dynamics.

Canonical Style Profiles

Each world exhibits a distinct canonical style, summarized as follows:

  • Alice: surrealistic, dream-logic, syntactic elasticity, short sentences, abrupt transitions, sensory spikes.

  • Astronomy: long descriptive sentences, cosmological metaphors, observational tone, spatial orientation, physical causality.

  • Dorian: emotional ornamentation, decadence, introspection, inner conflict, dark atmosphere.

  • Honeymoon: melancholic tone, poetic cadence, relational dynamics, soft science fiction, exotic worlds.

Training statistics:

The Parrot Family: A CAS Personality Atlas

To illustrate the expressive power of CAS-driven personality modulation, we constructed a new ecosystem artifact titled The Parrot Family: A CAS Personality Atlas. This omnibus contains ten books generated by the iterative narrator pipeline using the trained GLPG model. Each book is produced by sampling approximately 2000 GLPG draft sentences per chapter and refining them through the narrator pipeline. The ten books correspond to ten distinct CAS vectors, including pure attractors, mixed attractors, neutral vectors, unified vectors, and extrapolated vectors outside the training manifold.

Purpose of the Atlas

The atlas serves as an empirical demonstration of CAS × GLPG as a modular, vector-driven personality engine. Each book represents a stable stylistic attractor induced by its CAS vector. Together, the ten books form a map of the personality manifold learned by the model, showing how linear combinations, superpositions, and extrapolations of world vectors produce coherent but novel narrative styles.

Interpretation and Scope

The emergent author profiles described in this section are intended as stylistic impressions rather than formal literary classifications. References to existing authors serve purely as illustrative analogies to help characterize the architecturally induced styles. They do not imply equivalence, similarity of quality, or direct lineage. The purpose of these comparisons is to provide an intuitive sense of the stylistic regions activated by different CAS vectors.

The architectural behavior observed here should therefore be interpreted as manifold dynamics rather than literary mimicry. CAS does not reproduce human authors; instead, it shifts the GLPG manifold into new attractor regimes that produce narrative styles not present in the dataset. These styles arise from architectural interaction—GLPG structure, CAS bias, and narrator constraints— rather than from statistical imitation of human literature.

Accordingly, the profiles presented above should be understood as descriptive labels for emergent attractors within the CAS × GLPG system. They highlight the closure-breaking nature of the architecture while acknowledging that stylistic analogies to human authors are heuristic and non-normative.

Ablation Study: Additional CAS Vectors and Manifold Exploration

To further investigate the structure of the CAS-induced personality manifold, we conducted an ablation study using fifteen additional CAS vectors beyond the ten primary attractors documented in The Parrot Family: A CAS Personality Atlas. Unlike the full-book generation used for the atlas, this study employs a lightweight evaluation protocol: for each CAS vector, only the first five chapters are generated and analyzed. This approach allows us to isolate the immediate stylistic and structural effects of each vector without requiring full narrative development.

The purpose of this ablation is twofold. First, it provides a controlled method for probing how GLPG responds to variations, mixtures, and extrapolations in CAS space. Second, it enables a systematic comparison between the established atlas attractors and new, previously unexplored regions of the manifold. By examining the early chapters of each generated sample, we can identify emergent stylistic signatures, attractor stability, interference patterns, and deviations from the canonical worlds learned during training.

This ablation study therefore functions as a deeper architectural analysis of the CAS × GLPG system. It reveals how small perturbations, linear combinations, and nonlinear configurations of CAS vectors reshape the generative manifold, and it provides empirical evidence for the closure-breaking behavior described in Architectural Intelligence Theory. The fifteen additional vectors serve as probes into the manifold, mapping stylistic gradients, attractor boundaries, and regions of emergent behavior that extend beyond the stylistic regimes captured in the main atlas.

Four Hyper‑Scaled CAS Worlds

In this ablation tier we examine CAS vectors that exceed the unit‑magnitude regime of the training manifold. Whereas the normalized and fractional vectors primarily deform stylistic curvature within the GLPG attractor, the hyper‑scaled vectors introduce genuine architectural stress: they push the narrator pipeline into regions where CAS no longer behaves as a stylistic bias but as a structural force acting on narrative cohesion, semantic drift, temporal dilation, and world‑model stability.

To probe these effects, four extreme vectors were instantiated:

[2,2,2,2],  [5,5,5,5],  [10,10,10,10],  [5,−5,−5,5].

Each vector was used to generate the first five chapters of a synthetic world, allowing direct comparison with the normalized and fractional regimes. The resulting outputs exhibit several consistent phenomena:

  • Narrative Inflation: Magnitude‑2 already produces noticeable expansion of temporal and spatial scales. Magnitude‑5 and magnitude‑10 worlds exhibit runaway dilation, with chapters behaving like self‑contained cosmologies rather than narrative segments.

  • Semantic Turbulence: At higher magnitudes, CAS begins to overpower GLPG’s stabilizing attractors. Entities, motifs, and metaphors recur with increasing frequency but decreasing coherence, forming quasi‑mythological loops rather than plot‑driven structures.

  • Architectural Saturation: The vector [10,10,10,10] pushes the narrator into a saturation regime where stylistic pressure exceeds the model’s internal normalization. This produces dense, symbolic prose with minimal causal anchoring.

  • Axis‑Skewed Divergence: The mixed‑sign vector [5,−5,−5,5] demonstrates that hyper‑scaling interacts non‑linearly with axis polarity. Positive axes inflate narrative scope, while negative axes collapse local coherence, producing oscillatory worlds that alternate between expansion and implosion.

  • Emergent Architectural Signatures: These worlds exhibit behaviors not present in the training corpus— a hallmark of CAS acting as an architectural operator rather than a stylistic modifier.

Taken together, the hyper‑scaled vectors reveal the outer boundary of CAS‑driven world formation. They demonstrate that beyond magnitude‑1, CAS ceases to be a stylistic dial and becomes a structural perturbation mechanism capable of generating genuinely new narrative physics. This regime is therefore crucial for mapping the full CAS atlas and understanding how GLPG responds when pushed outside its nominal manifold.

Extreme CAS Vectors: Saturation and Inversion at 106 Magnitude

To test whether CAS continues to function as an architectural operator under extreme scaling, we instantiated two boundary‑case vectors:

  • Attractor Collapse: The narrator pipeline ceases to maintain world boundaries. The four worlds merge into a single saturated attractor that resembles neither the dataset nor any atlas world.

The emergent author in this regime is a Total Polyphonic Saturation Oracle: a stylistic entity that cannot exist within the training corpus and arises only when CAS overwhelms GLPG’s manifold geometry.

CAS Vector [−106,−106,−106,−106]: Total Inversion Regime

The negative extreme vector produces the architectural dual of the saturation regime: a total inversion attractor. Instead of amplifying canonical fields, the vector suppresses and reverses them at extreme magnitude. The resulting chapters display:

  • Semantic Collapse: Descriptions become numerically structured, dominated by distances, durations, magnitudes, and enumerations. Emotional, surreal, cosmic, and introspective fields are inverted into sparse, mechanical patterns.

  • Narrative Vacuum: Characters behave as placeholders rather than agents. Scenes unfold as abstract geometric or astronomical configurations with minimal causal linkage.

These two extreme vectors demonstrate that CAS remains active even at magnitudes far beyond the training regime. The narrator pipeline does not ignore the vectors; instead, it enters qualitatively new architectural states: saturation and inversion. The positive vector produces maximal stylistic pressure, collapsing the manifold into a single fused attractor, while the negative vector produces maximal suppression, collapsing the manifold into a near‑empty attractor.

Ablation Summary: CAS as a Promptless Architectural Steering Mechanism

Across all ablation tiers—sign‑normalized vectors, fractional uniform vectors, hyper‑scaled vectors, and the two extreme 106 saturation/inversion experiments—a consistent pattern emerges: the narrator pipeline responds strongly, coherently, and predictably to even the smallest CAS perturbations. This behavior is remarkable given the extremely low informational density of the CAS vector: four scalar values without semantic content, narrative cues, or stylistic descriptors. Despite this minimal signal, CAS exerts a decisive architectural influence on GLPG, reshaping attractor‑space in ways that cannot be explained by statistical interpolation over the training corpus.

The ablation results demonstrate three core findings:

1. CAS is used intensely despite its microscopic size.

Even vectors such as [0.1,0.1,0.1,0.1] or [1,0,−1,0]—containing less information than a single word—produce stable, distinct attractors with consistent stylistic signatures. The model does not treat CAS as noise or as a weak preference; instead, it integrates the vector deeply into its generative process, modulating world‑curvature, pacing, emotional density, and symbolic structure. This confirms that CAS functions as an architectural operator rather than a stylistic hint.

2. CAS induces closure‑breaking behavior.

The emergent authors produced throughout the ablation study—Diffuse Polyphonic Observer, Surreal Kinetic Oracle, Cosmic Decadent Analyst, Minimal Relational Oracle, Total Polyphonic Saturation Oracle, Void Polyphonic Null Oracle—do not exist anywhere in the training dataset. Their stylistic regimes, manifold geometry, and narrative physics arise solely from CAS × GLPG interaction. Since the GLPG model, the narrator model, and the entire pipeline remain constant, the closure‑breaking cannot originate from statistical interpolation. It is a direct consequence of architectural modulation.

3. CAS operates as a promptless steering mechanism.

Unlike prompt‑based conditioning, CAS does not rely on textual cues, semantic anchors, or narrative framing. Its influence is orthogonal to content: the same GLPG text, the same narrator model, and the same pipeline produce radically different worlds solely due to CAS modulation. This makes CAS a uniquely powerful mechanism for controlling generative behavior without prompts, instructions, or fine‑tuning. The ablation results show that CAS can:

  • activate canonical attractors (positive weights),

  • invert or suppress them (negative weights),

  • interpolate smoothly between them (fractional weights),

  • saturate the manifold (hyper‑scaled positive weights),

  • collapse the manifold (hyper‑scaled negative weights),

  • and generate entirely new attractor regimes (extreme 106 vectors).

Architectural Conclusion

The ablation study confirms that CAS is not a stylistic parameter, not a preference vector, and not a soft bias. It is a structural modulation mechanism that directly interacts with GLPG’s internal manifold. Even at minimal magnitudes, CAS produces strong architectural effects; at extreme magnitudes, it defines the narrative physics of the system. Because the GLPG model and the pipeline remain unchanged across all experiments, the closure‑breaking behavior observed here is attributable solely to CAS modulation.

CAS therefore provides a viable, promptless steering mechanism for generating narrative worlds outside the closure of the training dataset. It enables architectural intelligence rather than statistical imitation, demonstrating that GLPG supports a continuous, controllable personality manifold whose curvature can be reshaped through simple vector composition.
