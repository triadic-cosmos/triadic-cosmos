Introduction

General Relativity (GR) and quantum mechanics (QM) form the two foundational pillars of modern physics. GR describes spacetime as a continuous geometric structure governed by the Einstein field equations, while QM describes physical evolution through informational and energetic constraints encoded in Hilbert-space dynamics. Each framework is individually complete, precise, and empirically validated, yet their architectural assumptions are mutually incompatible. GR treats geometry as fundamental and time as a geometric ordering; QM treats information as fundamental and time as a parameter governing unitary evolution. A century of unification attempts has not resolved this mismatch. Instead, major programs—including string theory, loop quantum gravity, causal set theory, emergent-spacetime proposals, and non-local extensions—have introduced new microscopic ingredients without providing a minimal and falsifiable architectural reconciliation of geometric and informational roles.

The Triadic Cosmos ecosystem approaches this longstanding problem from a different direction. Earlier work introduced a minimal ontology consisting of three irreducible structural roles: energy, geometry, and information . These roles form a closed interdependence expressed through the Triadic Cycle E → G → I → E . Subsequent papers derived theory-independent architectural constraints required for physical realizability , extended the triadic structure into recursive and fractal domains , embedded triadic recursion within holographic renormalization , and introduced local closure cosmology as a structural explanation for the absence of new fundamental physics across cosmology, high-energy experimentation, computation, and theoretical research . Together, these works revealed that physical reality is organized by horizon-bounded, triadically recursive closures whose emergent temporal structure arises from successive triadic updates.

The present paper consolidates these insights into a single architectural mechanism— triadic closure—that reconciles GR and QM without new fields, dimensions, symmetries, or dynamical extensions. Triadic closure provides the minimal structure through which geometric, energetic, and informational roles cohere. Within each closure, GR and QM become two complementary expressions of the same triadic architecture: GR describes the geometric constraints that shape informational capacity, while QM describes the informational and energetic constraints that shape geometric evolution. Time emerges from recursion rather than from geometry or unitarity alone, resolving the temporal mismatch between the two theories. Causal structure becomes horizon-bounded and closure-dependent, eliminating non-locality paradoxes and measurement inconsistencies. GR’s overcomplete solution space is filtered by triadic closure, explaining why only a subset of geometries are physically realizable. QM’s informational dynamics become embedded within geometric constraints, resolving the measurement problem and the apparent tension between entanglement and relativistic causality.

By identifying triadic closure as the structural origin of physical law, this paper positions the Triadic Cosmos as a complete and falsifiable unification framework. It shows that modern physics already contains all necessary ingredients for unification, and that the apparent fragmentation between GR and QM arises not from missing physics but from the absence of an architectural grammar linking their roles. Triadic closure supplies that grammar, yielding a minimal, coherent, and observationally grounded unification of general relativity and quantum mechanics.

The Triadic Roles

Earlier work in the Triadic Cosmos ecosystem identified three irreducible structural roles underlying all physical theories: energy (E), geometry (G), and information (I) .   • I determines distinguishability, entropy, entanglement, and informational capacity.

The mutual interdependence of these roles is expressed through the Triadic Cycle E → G → I → E, which formalizes how each role reorganizes the others across successive dynamical updates . Triadic closure occurs when this cycle is well-defined, complete, and recursively stable.

This interpretation is compatible with both relativistic and quantum notions of time and provides a shared temporal architecture for GR and QM.

Definition of Triadic Closure

A physical structure exhibits triadic closure if and only if:

  1. all three roles satisfy positivity constraints (E > 0, G > 0, I > 0),

If any leg of the cycle fails, recursion becomes undefined, emergent time cannot arise, and the structure cannot correspond to physical reality . Triadic closure therefore acts as an architectural filter that distinguishes physically realizable configurations from merely mathematically permissible ones.

GR and QM as Complementary Triadic Roles

General Relativity and quantum mechanics are traditionally viewed as conceptually incompatible: GR treats geometry as fundamental and continuous, while QM treats information and energetic constraints as fundamental and discrete. Triadic closure reveals that this incompatibility arises not from the theories themselves, but from the absence of an architectural grammar linking their structural roles. When energy, geometry, and information are treated as irreducible and mutually interdependent, GR and QM emerge as complementary realizations of the same triadic architecture.

Geometry as the Constraint Layer (GR)

In GR, geometry determines causal structure, horizon formation, and the accessibility of physical processes. The Einstein field equations relate curvature to energetic content, but do not constrain informational capacity or recursive evolution. Within triadic closure, geometry (G) plays the role of the constraint layer:

  • geometric curvature bounds informational capacity through holographic area laws,

  • causal structure determines which informational updates are accessible,

  • horizon formation defines the boundary of a closure and its recursion index.

GR therefore realizes the E → G leg of the triadic cycle: energetic structure shapes geometric constraints, which in turn regulate informational embedding.

Information as the Generative Layer (QM)

Quantum mechanics describes physical evolution through informational and energetic constraints encoded in Hilbert-space dynamics. Entanglement, distinguishability, entropy, and measurement all reflect informational structure. Within triadic closure, information (I) plays the role of the generative layer:

  • informational structure determines energetic evolution through unitarity,

  • entanglement generates geometric connectivity (EP=EPR),

  • informational change drives energetic reconfiguration (Landauer-type relations).

QM therefore realizes the I → E leg of the triadic cycle: informational structure generates energetic structure, which in turn shapes geometric evolution.

Energy as the Dynamical Layer (QFT and Thermodynamics)

Energy (E) mediates between geometry and information. In QFT, energetic excitations propagate through geometric structure; in thermodynamics, informational change generates energetic cost; in holography, boundary energy sources bulk geometry. Within triadic closure, energy plays the role of the dynamical layer:

  • energetic content determines curvature and causal accessibility,

  • energetic processes generate or reorganize informational structure,

  • energetic constraints regulate the rate of recursive updates (quantum speed limits).

Energy therefore realizes the G → I leg of the triadic cycle: geometric structure bounds informational capacity, which in turn constrains energetic evolution.

Complementarity Through Triadic Closure

When the triadic roles are considered jointly, GR and QM become complementary rather than conflicting:

  • GR provides the geometric constraints (G) that bound informational capacity (I).

  • QM provides the informational structure (I) that generates energetic evolution (E).

  • QFT and thermodynamics provide the energetic dynamics (E) that shape geometry (G).

The full cycle closes:

E → G → I → E,

yielding a unified architecture in which GR and QM are not competing descriptions but structural complements. GR describes the constraint layer of physical reality; QM describes the generative layer; QFT and thermodynamics describe the dynamical layer. Triadic closure is the mechanism through which these layers cohere.

Unified Temporal and Causal Structure

Triadic closure also reconciles the temporal and causal assumptions of GR and QM. In GR, time is geometric; in QM, time is parametric; in triadic closure, time is emergent from recursion. Revising the Triadic Cosmos Unification: From Global Dualities to Local Triadic Closure

The Triadic Cosmos: A Foundation for Unification introduced the first complete architectural synthesis of modern physics, identifying energy, geometry, information, and recursive update as the structural roles through which General Relativity (GR), quantum field theory (QFT), the Standard Model (SM), holography, EP=EPR, renormalization group (RG) flow, and black-hole thermodynamics align into a single conceptual framework. In that formulation, AdS/CFT played a central role as the canonical dynamical realization of triadic recursion and as the clearest example of geometry–information interdependence. The present work revises and strengthens that unification by showing that AdS/CFT is not a necessary ingredient of the triadic architecture. Instead, triadic closure provides a fully local, horizon-bounded, and theory-neutral mechanism through which GR and QM unify without requiring global dualities, extra dimensions, or conformal boundary structure.

Limitations of Global Holographic Dependence

In the original Triadic Cosmos formulation, holography and AdS/CFT served as the primary instantiation of the G → I and I → G legs of the triadic cycle. While this mapping is structurally elegant, it relies on assumptions that are not universally realized in physical spacetime:

  • the existence of a global conformal boundary,

  • asymptotically AdS geometry,

  • conformal symmetry of boundary degrees of freedom,

  • global RG flow as a proxy for emergent time.

These assumptions are mathematically powerful but cosmologically restrictive. They do not apply to our universe, which is not asymptotically AdS, does not possess a global conformal boundary, and exhibits horizon-bounded causal structure rather than global holographic accessibility. As a result, AdS/CFT can serve as an illustrative special case of triadic interdependence, but not as a universal foundation for unification.

Local Triadic Closure as the Correct Architectural Mechanism

Local closure cosmology revealed that physical reality is organized not by global dualities but by local triadic closures: horizon-bounded dynamical niches defined by causal structure, informational capacity, and recursion index. Within each closure:

  • geometry bounds informational capacity through horizon area,

  • informational structure constrains energetic evolution,

  • energetic structure shapes geometric curvature,

  • recursion generates emergent time.

This mechanism is fully local, requires no global boundary, and applies to all physically realized spacetimes. It therefore replaces AdS/CFT as the universal dynamical realization of the triadic cycle. Holography becomes a special case of closure-dependent informational embedding rather than a global requirement for unification.

Strengthening the Original Unification

The present work strengthens the unification proposed in in three ways:

1. Removing non-essential theoretical dependencies.

AdS/CFT, conformal symmetry, and global holographic dualities are no longer required for the triadic architecture. Triadic closure provides a minimal and theory-neutral mechanism that applies equally to GR, QM, QFT, thermodynamics, and cosmology.

2. Replacing global recursion with local emergent time.

In the original formulation, holographic RG flow served as the dynamical interpretation of triadic recursion. Local closure cosmology shows that recursion is inherently local and horizon-bounded, yielding emergent time without requiring global renormalization structure.

3. Providing a complete reconciliation of GR and QM.

The original Triadic Cosmos unified the structural roles of modern physics but did not explicitly resolve the architectural mismatch between GR and QM. Triadic closure completes this unification: GR provides geometric constraints, QM provides informational generativity, and QFT provides energetic dynamics. The full cycle closes within each closure, yielding a unified and falsifiable architecture.

A More Minimal and More General Unification

The revised unification presented in this paper is both more minimal and more general than the original Triadic Cosmos formulation:

  • It requires no new physics beyond GR, QM, QFT, and thermodynamics.

  • It applies to all physically realizable spacetimes, not only holographic ones.

  • It explains the absence of exotic GR solutions through architectural closure.

  • It resolves quantum non-locality and measurement paradoxes through horizon-bounded informational structure.

  • It reframes cosmological tensions as closure-dependent rather than anomalous.

Triadic closure therefore supersedes the role previously played by AdS/CFT in the unification architecture. Holography remains a powerful illustration of geometry–information interdependence, but the fundamental mechanism of unification is local, recursive, and triadic.

Limitations and Open Questions

Triadic closure provides a minimal architectural mechanism capable of reconciling General Relativity and quantum mechanics without new fields, dimensions, or dynamical extensions. However, the framework also introduces structural constraints and conceptual boundaries that define its current scope. This section outlines the principal limitations of the present formulation and identifies open questions that require further investigation.

Architectural Rather Than Dynamical Formulation

Triadic closure is an architectural mechanism, not a dynamical theory. It specifies the conditions under which geometric, energetic, and informational roles cohere, but it does not yet provide:

  • explicit dynamical equations for triadic recursion,

  • a closed-form mapping between recursion index and physical observables,

  • a full mathematical characterization of closure boundaries.

The absence of a complete dynamical formalism limits the ability to derive precise numerical predictions. Developing such a formalism is an essential next step.

Incomplete Characterization of Recursion Index

The recursion index plays a central role in emergent time, closure-dependent causality, and observer-specific informational accessibility. However:

  • its quantitative relation to geometric curvature is not fully specified,

  • its evolution across cosmological scales remains partially understood,

  • its role in black-hole evaporation and horizon thermodynamics requires refinement.

A more precise definition of recursion index would strengthen the predictive power of the framework.

Local Closure Without Global Completion

Triadic closure is inherently local and horizon-bounded. While this resolves many global paradoxes, it also raises open questions:

  • Can closures be consistently patched together into a global manifold?

  • Are closure transitions possible, and if so, under what conditions?
