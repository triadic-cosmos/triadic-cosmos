Imaging shows clear dust emission from the Sun-facing side and a radiation-pressure tail, consistent with high-entropy interstellar debris. Analyses of its dynamical and chemical properties suggest that 3I/ATLAS likely originated in the outskirts of an old stellar system, potentially older than the Sun. No technosignatures or artificial characteristics have been detected.

Appendix: Structural Impossibility of Time Travel in Dual Time

Dual Time provides a structural reinterpretation of physical temporality that renders classical notions of time travel—both forward and backward—architecturally impossible. This conclusion does not arise from relativistic constraints, energetic infeasibility, or paradox avoidance, but from the fundamental temporal grammar of the Triadic Cosmos: the coexistence of universal electromagnetic time (tEM) and closure-dependent material time (tmaterial).

Global Electromagnetic Time Cannot Be Reversed or Replayed

Electromagnetic time is closure-transversal and universal. Photons and gravitational waves propagate across closures while preserving coherence, causal ordering, and frequency structure. Because tEM is not generated by recursion but carried by propagation, it has no internal degrees of freedom that could be reversed, rewound, or reparameterized. Any attempt to “travel” in tEM would require altering the causal structure of electromagnetic propagation itself, which is structurally impossible within a horizon-bounded closure.

Material Time Is Closure-Bound and Non-Exportable

Material time arises from triadic recursion within a closure. Its rate is determined by entropy, recursion index, and local dynamical structure. Because tmaterial is generated internally rather than propagated externally, it cannot be detached from the closure that produces it. A biological organism, physical object, or artificial system cannot carry its material time into another closure without undergoing a recursion-index mismatch that redefines its temporal rate. Material time is therefore non-exportable, non-reversible, and non-transversal.

Relativistic Time Dilation Does Not Enable Time Travel

Relativistic time dilation modifies geometric accessibility and energetic flux, but it does not alter the structural origin of time. Time dilation changes the rate at which tmaterial unfolds within a closure, but it does not permit reversal, replay, or traversal of temporal states. Dual Time therefore reinterprets relativistic time dilation as a geometric modulation of recursion rate, not as a mechanism for temporal displacement.

Closure Overlap Does Not Permit Temporal Leakage

Closures may overlap through electromagnetic or gravitational coupling, but such overlap does not permit temporal exchange. tEM remains universal and forward-directed; tmaterial remains closure-bound and emergent. Overlap allows information transfer but not temporal traversal. Voyager demonstrates this explicitly: its material time diverges from Earth’s, yet its electromagnetic communication remains coherent. No temporal leakage occurs.

Temporal Paradoxes Become Structurally Undefined

Classical paradoxes—grandfather paradox, bootstrap paradox, causal loops—presuppose a single, globally navigable timeline. Dual Time eliminates this assumption. Because tEM is universal and irreversible, and tmaterial is closure-dependent and non-transversal, paradoxical temporal configurations cannot be instantiated. There is no global timeline to loop back into, no closure-independent material time to revisit, and no mechanism for exporting recursion states across closures.

Implications for Space Travel and Temporal Engineering

Long-duration space travel requires explicit temporal engineering to preserve biological stability. Biological organisms depend on closure-specific tmaterial anchored by terrestrial electromagnetic spectra and gravitational structure. In interplanetary or interstellar environments, these anchors change, altering recursion index and subjective temporal flow. Artificial systems remain stable in tEM, but biological systems do not. Temporal engineering is therefore necessary for biological viability, but it does not enable time travel; it preserves temporal coherence rather than modifying temporal direction.

Conclusion

Within the Dual Time architecture, time travel is structurally impossible. Electromagnetic time cannot be reversed or replayed; material time cannot be exported or traversed; relativistic effects cannot produce temporal displacement; closure overlap cannot generate temporal leakage; and classical paradoxes become architecturally undefined. Dual Time therefore provides a unified structural explanation for the empirical absence of time travel across physics, cosmology, biology, and computation.
